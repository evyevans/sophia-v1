# Design decisions

Floatglass is a local crypto research edition. Its deterministic checks use full asset identity, UTC market data, source lineage, neutral research lenses and explicit risk blockers. The project does not infer investor endorsements or promise performance.

The research workflow includes a local report, an optional public BTC/USD market collector, a focused agent profile, a savable skill source and an optional routine template. The evaluator is independent of a model. No execution, wallet signing, messaging, portfolio ledger or financial backtest is provided.
